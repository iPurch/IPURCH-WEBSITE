Process optimisation

Diagnose and redesign your procurement processes

We map your Source-to-Pay processes, spot friction points and broken flows, then define a shorter, more reliable target journey. Shorter cycles, lighter admin workload.

Automation

Automate what doesn't need to be manual

Approval workflows, automatic invoice matching, catalogues and ordering rules: no more re-keying or chasing. Your teams focus on buying, not paperwork.

Supplier onboarding

Suppliers onboarded quickly and cleanly

Supplier portal, registration and qualification questionnaires, compliance checks (legal documents, ESG, risk), approval workflow and automatic creation in the ERP. No more incomplete records or email chasing.
